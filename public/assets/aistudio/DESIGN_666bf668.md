## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a dark luxury editorial and high-fashion magazine aesthetic. Drawing inspiration from premium print publications, haute horlogerie, and architectural portfolios, it balances high-contrast brutalist scale with refined metallic precision.

### Target Audience & Persona
Engineered for senior engineering executives, high-caliber tech founders, gallery curators, and premium design agencies seeking elite technical and aesthetic capability. The emotional response is one of gravitas, architectural discipline, and quiet opulence.

### Design Movements & Visual Language
- **Dark Editorial Print:** Monochromatic canvas depth layered with monumental condensed display headlines and airy micro-typography.
- **Warm Metallic Precision:** Strict restraint using obsidian charcoal foundations with champagne cream type and antique gold accents.
- **Physical Depth & Planar Layering:** Multi-layered spatial hierarchy (backdrop display lettering, subject cutout middle plane, and elevated foreground captions).
- **Ornamental Restraint:** Minimalist punctuation using crisp 4-point star glyphs (`✦`), razor-thin hairline borders, and hand-inked signature flourishes.

## Colors

The palette operates under a strict, unyielding dark color space. Avoid pure black or clinical cool grays; every tone is warm-cast with obsidian, taupe, and bronze undertones.

### Application Guidelines
- **Base Canvas (`#0D0C0A`):** The foundational substrate. All full-bleed sections, viewport wrappers, and page backgrounds rest here.
- **Elevated Surfaces (`#151310`, `#1D1A15`):** Reserved for bento panels, showcase modules, and elevated overlays. Subtle hover states transition smoothly between these two values.
- **Text & Foregrounds:** Primary readable narrative copy uses cream white (`#F4EFE6`), while metadata, timestamps, and secondary captions use warm taupe (`#9A9284`).
- **Metallic Hierarchy:** Antique Gold (`#C5A065`) drives structural accents, index numbers, glyphs, and active states. Champagne Light (`#E8D5B7`) is reserved for high-impact display headlines and primary callouts.

## Typography

The typography system relies on an intentional collision between monumental, condensed sans-serif titles and clinical, airy grotesque body copy.

### Rules of Usage
- **Anton (Headlines & Numerals):** Render all section titles, primary titles, hero displays, and index numbers in uppercase Anton. Keep line heights tight (`0.88` to `1.05`) to maintain typographic density.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body & Interface):** Handles all narrative prose, bento titles, and interface labeling.
- **Tracking Discipline:** Micro-labels, eyebrows, and category tags must be set with expansive tracking (`0.08em` to `0.25em`) and uppercase styling to evoke luxury branding.
- **Signature Flourish:** For unique personal signatures or editorial stamps, pair with an organic script font (such as *Alex Brush* or *Caveat*) set at `28px` with a `-4deg` slant, never used for informational text.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an editorial grid combining asymmetric bento blocks with strict structural alignments.

### Grid & Composition
- **Page Margin:** Fixed fluid padding of `5vw` horizontally across desktop viewports, down to `1.25rem` on mobile. Maximum container width is constrained to `1240px` and centered.
- **Desktop Bento Modules:** Multi-column arrangements using fractional proportions (e.g., `1.1fr 1.6fr 0.9fr` for indices or `repeat(4, 1fr)` for showcases).
- **Responsive Adaptation:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** Full asymmetric multi-column layouts with `1.75rem` (`28px`–`36px`) gutters.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** Grids collapse to 2 columns; typography scales via mobile variants.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** All bento panels stack into a single column (`1fr`); margins compress to safe padding; gutters lock to `1rem`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through planar contrast, hairline gold delineation, and delicate ambient luminescence rather than diffuse drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Gradients & Glows
- **Ambient Floor Glow:** Atmospheric background fields use subtle radial dissipation: `radial-gradient(circle at 50% 0%, rgba(197, 160, 101, 0.08), transparent 70%)`.
- **Card Highlights:** Elevated surfaces do not receive heavy box shadows. Hover states emit an ethereal gold perimeter glow: `0 0 24px rgba(197, 160, 101, 0.18)`.
- **Medallion Surfaces:** Interactive circular badges utilize an internal gradient fill transitioning from `rgba(197, 160, 101, 0.22)` to `rgba(197, 160, 101, 0.06)`.

### Layered Z-Index Architecture
In editorial spreads, depth is achieved by overlapping structural tiers:
1. **Background Layer (`z-index: 1`):** Large-scale typographic backdrop text.
2. **Subject Midground (`z-index: 2`):** Masked portrait or specimen photography fading into the base floor with a linear gradient.
3. **Foreground Layer (`z-index: 3`):** High-contrast micro-captions, hairpins, and interactive CTAs.

## Shapes

The design system maintains controlled geometric softened contours. Surfaces use balanced radii to soften the architectural brutalism of the condensed headlines.

### Radius System
- **Cards & Containers (`rounded-lg` / 16px):** Bento cards, timeline elements, and content panels.
- **Hero Containers (`rounded-xl` / 20px–24px):** Large full-section bento frames.
- **Embedded Media (12px–14px):** Project thumbnails and visual previews nested within parent cards.
- **Pills & Circles (Circular / 50%):** Tool medallions, avatar badges, status indicators, and floating social triggers.
- **Hairlines:** Structural borders use crisp 1px strokes; decorative accent rules feature fixed dimensions (e.g., `80px x 1px`).

## Components

### Buttons & Social Links
- **Primary Action Buttons:** Surface filled with antique gold (`#C5A065`), label in dark canvas (`#0D0C0A`) in bold tracked uppercase. On hover, background shifts to champagne (`#E8D5B7`) with a subtle transform scale of `1.03`.
- **Circular Icon Triggers:** `38px x 38px` circular containers with `1px solid var(--border-subtle)`. Icon glyph rendered in Antique Gold. Hover transitions background to Gold Light and icon to dark base with decelerated ease `cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)`.

### Bento & Portfolio Cards
- **Base Style:** Rendered in `#151310` with a 1px border of `rgba(197, 160, 101, 0.25)`.
- **Inner Padding:** Consistent `1.75rem` (`28px`).
- **Interactive State:** Hover elevates card surface to `#1D1A15`, scales preview imagery by `1.05`, and projects a subtle gold atmospheric outer glow.

### Section Dividers & Ornaments
- Horizontal hairpins spanning full container width: `1px solid rgba(197, 160, 101, 0.25)`.
- Center alignment interrupted with a solid 4-point star ornament (`✦`) rendered in `#C5A065` at `14px`–`18px`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Inputs:** Surface matching `#151310`, border `1px solid rgba(244, 239, 230, 0.08)`. Focus states illuminate the border with `#C5A065` and introduce an ambient `rgba(197, 160, 101, 0.18)` glow. Placeholder typography in taupe (`#9A9284`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Minimalist angular square or circular borders in `1px solid var(--border-subtle)` with Antique Gold center fill when active.

### Chips & Metadata Tags
- **Appearance:** Low-profile pills or soft rectangles (`rounded-sm`). Background set to translucent charcoal with `1px solid var(--border-faint)`.
- **Typography:** Set in tracked uppercase `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`11px`, `0.14em` letter-spacing) in `#9A9284`.
